Ulrika Jonsson, now in her mid-50s, describes herself as an
equal-opportunities dater who has found unexpected pleasure in the company
of younger men. After her 2019 divorce she tried online dating without
setting an age filter and discovered she liked the straightforward energy
and focus of men in their twenties and thirties. The youngest she has
gone out with was 26; the age difference, she says, has never created
problems in the bedroom, and her own sex drive remains as strong as ever
even while she takes HRT.

She is clear that the preference was not deliberate. Men closer to her
own age can carry unresolved history, while younger partners tend to
live more in the present. Conversation and compatibility still matter
more than any checklist of traits; she has never had a fixed type and
insists variety keeps things interesting. At the same time she rejects
any suggestion of ageism, inviting interest from all quarters.

Looking back, Jonsson says her fifties have brought a new confidence
and fearlessness. She feels sexier and more herself than she did in
her twenties or thirties, no longer concerned with external judgment,
and describes the decade as far more exciting than earlier ones. She
has been married three times and is the mother of four children.
