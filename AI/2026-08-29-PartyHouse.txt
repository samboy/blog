A Bellevue, Washington neighborhood experienced months of disruptive parties at a large mansion, with hundreds of people reportedly arriving every weekend. Neighbors repeatedly called police because of traffic, noise, crowds, and concerns that emergency vehicles could be blocked.

A police raid later revealed allegations that the mansion was being used as an OnlyFans-style content house tied to a human trafficking investigation. According to court documents and interviews with seven women, investigators allege that women were recruited with promises of large earnings from online adult-content platforms, but then had their accounts, earnings, and bank access controlled by the suspects.

### Main points

Months of disruption: Residents reported large parties every Friday and Saturday, crowded streets, excessive noise, and hundreds of visitors.

Frequent police calls: One neighbor estimated she called 911 nearly every weekend because the situation became a serious nuisance and potentially blocked emergency access.

Police raid: Bellevue police raided the mansion around 4:30 a.m. Thursday as part of a human trafficking investigation.

Evidence seized: Police reportedly found more than 300 cell phones, 50+ laptops, financial records, a notebook tracking women's names and earnings, and a “Content Plan” whiteboard, along with luxury vehicles.

Alleged exploitation: Investigators say seven women described being recruited for online adult-content creation and subsequently subjected to controlling behavior and physical abuse.

Financial control: Suspects allegedly took control of the women's account credentials and bank accounts. One woman said an account in her name generated nearly $230,000, but she never received the money.

Alleged coercion and abuse: Women reportedly said they were required to spend at least eight hours a day interacting with fans and additional hours producing content, sometimes using stimulants to keep working. Some alleged threats, beatings, restraint, and retaliation when they tried to leave.

Arrest: A man was arrested during the raid and is being held on $5 million bail. He had not yet been formally charged at the time of the report.

Neighborhood reaction: Residents who initially thought they were dealing with an unusually disruptive party house now believe something much more serious was happening. The neighborhood has become quiet again.

### Bottom line

What appeared to neighbors to be a recurring party house was allegedly the site of a sophisticated online adult-content operation involving human trafficking, financial exploitation, and abuse.
