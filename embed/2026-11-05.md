### The Blue Tsunami

As has been widely discussed in the news, Democrats have had incredible
gains in Tuesday’s elections.  Even *Texas* turned blue this election cycle,
something not seen for decades.  In fact, [this 2024 → 2026 shift is the 
biggest two year shift with voters since the 1930s, with people voting 
some 17% more blue in 2026 than they did 
in 2024](https://archive.ph/https://www.themotte.org/post/4059/culture-war-roundup-for-the-week/495417?context=8).

Republican Vice President JD Vance has an [excellent
summary](AI/2026-11-05-Vance.html) of what happened.  He says
that ending the Iran war is important, reducing inflation is also
important, as well as putting America first and reassessing our
relationship with Israel.  I generally agree with him; I have a friend who
I was talking to the day before the election; she asked me who to vote
for.  I told her to vote straight Blue (Democrat) because the war on Iran
was increasing gas prices, but to vote against the wealth tax, because
that passing would destroy California’s economy.

This is definitely *not* a mandate for leftist “culture war” causes.
Blue Washington state [looks to be passing an initiative forbidding biological
males on female sport
teams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Washington_Initiative_638), and
liberal California [looks to have voted against a wealth tax for 
billionaires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_California_Proposition_40).
As Clinton said in 1992, “It’s the economy, stupid”.  Indeed, Texas’s
Democrat governor-elect [doesn’t want a culture
war](AI/2026-11-05-Hinojosa.html).  As it turns out, [candidates endorsed by 
leftist Sanders did worse than candidates not endorsed by
Sanders](https://archive.ph/https://www.imightbewrong.org/p/how-leftists-and-moderates-did-in).

Point being, the Republicans went too far with an unwanted war in Iran.
Democrats have a chance to hold on to power by giving voters what they
want: That means improving the economy, *not* engaging in an unwanted
culture war and *not* trying to make America a leftist state.
