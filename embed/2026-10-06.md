can start a family with, and that includes women who have slept with

Here’s one [article](AI/2026-10-06-Bower.html), which looks at consent

is OK; it looks at how a woman is treated overall by someone
affects how she views sex with them.

There’s a quote

>a husband who believes, often with the backing of a church that teaches
>wifely submission, that sex is his right and her duty, and who treats her
>like a servant in the rest of their life too. It isn’t the commitment
>that makes the difference. It’s the degree to which he sees her as a
>person or as property.

Let’s look at what scripture has to say.  Let’s go to that part of
Ephesians which the radical left hates:

>Wives, be submissive to your own husbands, as to the Lord. For the
>husband is the head of the wife, and Christ also is the head of the
>church, being himself the savior of the body. But as the church
>is subject to Christ, so let the wives also be to their own husbands

Women don’t like being treated as slaves obviously, and Paul who 
wrote this passage agrees.  The above scripture continues:

>Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church,

>cleansed it by the washing of water with the word, that he might
>present the church to himself gloriously, not having spot or wrinkle or
>any such thing; but that it should be holy and without blemish. Even
>so husbands also ought to love their own wives as their own bodies. He

If men aren’t doing their side of the deal where wives are led by their
husbands, that is not a marriage following Christian principles.  Since
some men don’t follow both sides of the bargain where the husband is
