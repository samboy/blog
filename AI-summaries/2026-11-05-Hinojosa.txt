In a shift from partisan expectations, Texas Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa
used her first press conference to stress that her administration would
serve the entire state rather than only Democratic voters. She framed
the moment as one for unity, noting that Texans are weary of endless
culture wars and political fighting and want practical results instead.

She pointed to concrete collaboration already underway, highlighting her
longstanding partnership with Republican Representative Ernest Bailes
on public education. Describing him as one of her strongest allies on
schools—with little daylight between their positions—she announced
he would play a key leadership role on her transition team.

Hinojosa tied the approach directly to her campaign commitments,
saying the goal is simply to deliver for people across the political
spectrum. She made clear this would not be a Democratic-only government,
underscoring that she intends to govern for all Texans.
